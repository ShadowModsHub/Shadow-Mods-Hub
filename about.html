Our Story

Shadow Mods Hub was born out of a simple frustration — finding quality GTA mods was a nightmare. Broken links, sketchy sites, and hours of searching for a single working download. We decided to fix that.

What started as a small personal collection of GTA San Andreas mods quickly grew into a full community hub. We hand-pick every mod for quality, safety and compatibility before it ever appears on our site.

Today, Shadow Mods Hub serves tens of thousands of GTA players every month, spanning everything from anime character skins and realistic ENB shaders to custom maps and weapon packs — all completely free, no account required.

Our promise: every mod is scanned, tested and linked to a clean source. No malware, no fake downloads, no pop-up traps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

Are all mods free to download?
Yes, 100%. Every mod on Shadow Mods Hub is completely free. No account, no subscription, no hidden fees — ever.
How do I install a mod?
Most mods come with a README file. Generally: extract the zip, copy the files to your GTA game folder, and use a mod installer like CLEO or IMG Tool. Instructions vary per mod.
Can I submit my own mod?
Absolutely! Use the contact form above and select "Submit a Mod". Include a description, working download link and at least one screenshot. We review all submissions.
A download link is broken. What do I do?
Use the form above and select "Report Broken Link". Tell us the mod name and we'll fix or replace the link within 24 hours.
Do the mods work on mobile GTA?
Most mods are designed for the PC versions of GTA SA and Vice City. Mobile modding is limited and varies by device. Check each mod's description for compatibility notes.
How long does it take to get a reply?
We aim to reply to all emails and form submissions within 24–48 hours. For the fastest response, join our Discord server where the team is active daily.
